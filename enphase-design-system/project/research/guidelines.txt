Primary logo
lockup
Our logo is used in nearly all of our
communications. It is our stamp of
authenticity. It is most important that
it is reproduced consistently in a way
that it is clearly legible and identifiable.
It is vital that the correct logo is used
in each situation.
The horizontal version is our preferred
logo. It should always be the first choice
over the vertical logo.

Primary logo
lockup
The Enphase logo lockup is made of
two elements, the Logo mark and the
wordmark. The Enphase logo has been
trademarked. Please ensure that you
always use the logo with its registered
trademark. For specialty items, where the
trademark cannot be printed clearly, it is
permissible to omit the trademark symbol.

Gradients
We use several gradients to evoke
a sense of light traveling through an
environment. Each gradient takes
a cue from the sun’s position in the
sky at different times of day.
Our brand gradient is used most within
Enphase communications. Examples
of where our brand gradient may
appear are as a background element on
applications and within backdrops
of product images or illustrations.
In addition to our brand gradient, we
also use spotlight gradients, a sunrise
gradient and a sunset gradient. These
colors should be reserved for moments
within user interfaces and backdrops for
illustrations.
All gradient end-to-end points are
fixed to the container height and run
in a 90º vertical orientation.

Our typography
Enphase Visuelt is the primary
typeface for all visual communication
and should be used for most copy,
including body copy, headlines,
and CTAs.
For highly technical information,
detailed stats, section eyebrows,
and captions, Enphase uses T-Star Pro.
Its shared proportions and mono-
line stroke weight are reminiscent of
precision instruments such as calipers,
cameras, and microscopes.

Margins
To create a grid for a composition,
the first step consists in calculating
the margins.
For vertical and square compositions
we allocate 5% of the total width to
each side.
For horizontal compositions we allocate
5% of the total height.
For digital applications, please round the
5% value to the nearest whole number.
For example, if the width of a digital
application is 525px, 5% of 525px would
equal 26.25. Rounding the value to the
nearest whole number would create a
margin value of 26px

our margin is equal to 96px we would set
the rounded corner setting to 48px.
Following this rule will create consistency
whenever rounded corners appear.

created by misaligning Tile gutters
and/or using an asymmetrical grid
(grids containing 3 , 5 or 9 columns).
Also consider removing Tiles to create
white space. Doing so will allow
breathing room within the layout.
